# Book of sand

**The Book of Sand** is a fictional infinite book from Jorge Luis Borges’s story of the same name.

It is a very old, cloth-bound volume, surprisingly heavy for its size. The spine bears the words “Holy Writ” and, beneath them, “Bombay.” Its pages are printed in an unknown script, arranged in double columns like a traditional Bible, and are occasionally interrupted by illustrations. The pages are numbered with Arabic numerals, but the numbers appear in no logical or sequential order.

The book has neither a first page nor a last page. No matter how many leaves you turn, new pages continually appear from both the front and the back. Once a page is turned, it can never be found again. Its infinite nature is the reason it is called the Book of Sand: like grains of sand, it has no beginning and no end.
